Everything reached from the handle: the mode menu, Profiles, Adjust, the save sheet, Warnings and Information.

- **Mode menu.** Up to five 116 px discs on a circle, the active mode in `ink`, each with a 36 px icon over its `screen-label` name; the power disc at the centre is Standby. No list and no scrolling. Leave out Grind when there is no grinder and space the rest evenly.
- **Profiles.** One profile per screen: name, three fact cards (temperature, yield, time), the phase names and pagination dots. Chevron discs and swipes page; the change is a cut, not a slide.
- **Adjust.** A row picks the value, minus and plus change it, SAVE appears as the one action.
- **Save sheet.** Three choices, most likely first: SAVE, SAVE AS NEW, USE ONCE.
- **Warnings.** One card per warning with its level as a chip and a word (ERROR, WARN), then a sentence.
- **Information.** The QR card is white with black modules in every theme. Versions sit behind ABOUT. The same screen, headed JOIN THIS WI-FI with the machine's own network and SCAN TO SET UP, is the Wi-Fi setup screen (the DisplayStates card).

None of these screens draws the ring.

### Content

- Profile name on Profiles: `screen-value`, at most 12 characters; a longer name is cut at 11 with an ellipsis. The full name is on the web.
- Fact cards hold a value and unit of at most 5 characters: `93°`, `36 G`, `29 S`.
- The phase line is the names joined by middle dots, at most 25 characters; with more, write the count: "5 phases".
- A warning's sentence is at most 22 characters after its chip. At most three warnings a screen; a fourth shows as "+1 more".
- The Adjust title is the profile's name in capitals and, once anything changed, `· UNSAVED`.

### Touch

Mode discs are 116 px, chevron and pencil discs 56 px, pills 64 px tall, the row 56 px. A swipe left or right on Profiles does what the chevrons do; every swipe has a disc that does the same. Leaving a sheet is always a visible choice (USE ONCE, DONE), never only a swipe down.
