The web's four motions at their real durations: press, select, enter and leave, and a live reading.

Press and select use `duration-fast`. Things enter in `duration-base` on `ease-out` and leave in `duration-fast` on `ease-in`. A live reading never tweens: digits snap and segments recolour.

The same four cover everything added since: a Toast, a Menu and a Dialog enter and leave like the sheet here, the Loader steps instead of sweeping, and hover is a fill change with no movement. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` every transition and the Loader's steps are removed; nothing here is needed to understand the page.
