The dashboard on desktop: a sidebar tile and a 12 column bento grid, with the mode bar floating at the bottom centre.

The Dial tile spans 5 columns and 2 rows, the ActionBanner 4, the small StatTiles 3 each, Setup 4, the shot chart 7 and recent shots 5. Every tile is the same component as on the phone, only wider. The ActionBanner stretches to its row and sets its label at the bottom left.

The shot chart labels each line at its end with its last value and unit, and its legend Chips carry a sample of each line: solid for pressure and temperature, dashed for flow. The 36 px Flush button is a desktop size; on a touch screen it grows to 44 px by itself. Times follow Content: `Today 14:41`, never `02:41 PM`.
